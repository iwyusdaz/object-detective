# Alignment matrix

Objective → in-world activity → feedback → assessment item. `scripts/check-content.mjs` checks that every objective has practice, feedback and assessment in content/.

| Objective | Practice (tutorial, Case 1) | Activity (Case 2) | Feedback | Assessment item |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Observe precisely | 4 findings on the Morse-Vail key with tool hints; 8 classify items | 10 findings across 4 objects, no tool hints; 20 classify items | Immediate why-feedback per statement | Case 2 classification score (debrief); transfer statements ts1–ts3; pre-check pre-1 |
| 2 Source a document | Volunteer card sourcing note; modelled "How a historian sources this" | Records drawer with "who / when / why" for 4 real records and 2 fictional docs; sourcing questions sq-memo, sq-record | Why-feedback per sourcing answer; historian's sourcing behind a disclosure | sq-memo, sq-record; pre-check pre-2 |
| 3 Corroborate | Pin evidence to T1–T3 (supports / contradicts / unclear) | Pin evidence to M1–M6; red herring (relay serial number 589) | Debrief: expert key evidence per claim, "key evidence linked" | Case 2 "key evidence linked" score; pre-check pre-3 |
| 4 Write a calibrated label | Verdicts and confidence on T1–T3 (including "can't tell yet"); label writer with starters and exemplar | Verdicts and confidence on M1–M6; case label | Debrief: expert verdict and reasoning per claim, calibration (Brier), "very sure but wrong" list; exemplar comparison | Case 2 verdict score and calibration; rubric self-check; transfer claims X1–X3 and label sentence; pre-check pre-4 |

Pre-check (welcome) and transfer (chapter 4) together give a before/after picture for the learner. Neither is used as a research measure.
