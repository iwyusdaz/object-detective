# Personas

These personas are design tools taken from the plan. They are not real people and not research participants.

| Persona | Starting point | Need | How the design responds |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Amara, 14** (grade 9 student) | Treats labels as fact | A model for questioning sources | Tutorial hints name the tool to use. The volunteer's card is sourced before it is believed. Why-feedback on every classification |
| **Joel, 19** (first-year undergraduate) | Writes overconfident claims | Practice with hedged, evidence-linked language | Confidence ratings plus a calibration score. "Very sure but wrong on …" in the debrief. Uncertainty criterion in the rubric. Sentence starters such as "We cannot yet say whether …" |
| **Mr. Haddad** (secondary history teacher) | 40-minute period, mixed devices | A lesson plan, discussion questions, the historical-thinking link | `design/teacher-guide.md`. Text mode for low-end devices. Learners can export their record as JSON |
| **Priya** (museum educator) | Online pre/post-visit activity | Real collection objects, honest licensing | Real CC0 Smithsonian scans linked to their records. Fiction clearly labelled. Runs in any browser with no accounts |
