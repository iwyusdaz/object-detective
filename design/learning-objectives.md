# Learning objectives

By the end of the game, learners will be able to:

1. **Observe precisely.** Describe an artifact's material, construction, wear, marks and inscriptions, and correctly classify statements about it as *observation* or *inference* (target: at least 80 % of classifications correct in Case 2 and the transfer task).
2. **Source a document.** For a given document, state who made it, when and why, and judge how much weight it can bear for a specific claim (sourcing questions in Case 2).
3. **Corroborate.** Link physical evidence and archival records to a claim as *supports / contradicts / unclear*, and spot agreements and conflicts (key evidence linked correctly for at least 4 of 6 claims in Case 2).
4. **Write a calibrated label.** Give each claim a verdict (*confirmed / refuted / can't tell yet*) with a confidence rating, and write a public label that cites evidence and hedges where the evidence is weak (verdicts plus calibration in Case 2; label self-checked against the 4-criterion rubric).

The 80 % and 4-of-6 targets are design targets for the scoring display. They are not measured results; no learners have played this yet.
